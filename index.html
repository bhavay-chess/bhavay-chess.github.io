Chess Mechanics

This engine strictly follows standard rules:

  • Castling: Unhindered move between King & Rook.
  • En Passant: Pawn capturing rule.
  • Pawn Promotion: Choice of Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.

Strategy Tips

  • Center Control: Command key central squares early.
  • Piece Development: Move Knights and Bishops into key positions.
  • Protect Your King: Castle early for defense.
